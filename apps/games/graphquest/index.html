COURIER FIELD GUIDE

Read the roads.

A stop is a vertex. A road is an edge. Its weight tells you distance (km) or a charge (RM), depending on your mission.

Tap a connected stop on the map or use the large next-stop buttons. Your route is a sequence such as A → B → D. Add every road weight along it.

Arrows mean one-way roads; striped roads are closed. Lines crossing between stops are not junctions. The drawing is not to scale.

A shortest route can use more roads than another route. A cheapest route may travel farther. Budget missions accept every valid route within the limit.

点击相连的地点来规划路线。看清楚目标是最短距离、最低费用，还是预算内送达。单行道要顺着箭头走，路线可以撤销和重试。

Pilih bucu yang bersambung. Jumlahkan pemberat tepi. Ikut arah anak panah dan elakkan jalan yang ditutup.

Keyboard: Tab to a stop, then Enter/Space. No dragging required. Pause or skip the van animation at any time. Hints never reduce completion XP.
