    HOW TO MAKE A GOOD BATCH

    Observe. Adjust. Discover.

    1. Read the test sample and the model. Find the constant k.
    2. Change x and, where shown, z. The factory, table and graph follow your settings.
    3. Match the order’s target and run the machine. Hints and retries never reduce your reward.

    The graph shows y versus x. When z exists it stays fixed at your current setting. The highlighted dot is your machine setting. Inputs use positive values; root stations offer square areas.

    先从样本找出常数 k，再调整 x 和 z 完成订单。正变：一起增减；反变：一个增加，另一个减少；联变：取决于两个变量的乘积。图像、表格和产量会跟着变化。

    Cari pemalar k daripada sampel. Laraskan x dan z untuk memenuhi pesanan. Graf menunjukkan y melawan x; nilai z dikekalkan.

    Use Tab, Enter, number entry or the +/− buttons. There is no timer. Settings offers reduced motion and backup/import. First-try success includes live mathematical help; it is not an exam score.
