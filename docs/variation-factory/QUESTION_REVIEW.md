## Interpretation and limits

V03–V04 assume identical crews with perfectly shared work, a fixed job and no overhead; they teach an ideal inverse model. V07–V08 coat square lids only, not entire 3D tins. V09 uses one perimeter ribbon, 4 times the side; V10 models a perimeter plus two side-length crossing strips, 6 times the side, without knot allowance. Root tasks use x as square area, never side length. V11–V12 explicitly model portions per equal box from fixed-size batches; more boxes reduce portions per box. Fractional portions are permitted while exploring.

The graph is y versus x; z is fixed at the currently selected value when present. Live output, graph and table make all tasks guided; first-try records are not unaided arithmetic evidence. Reports do not expose unattempted answer constants.
